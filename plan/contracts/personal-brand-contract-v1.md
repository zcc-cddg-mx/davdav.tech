# 1. Purpose

This document defines the personal branding strategy, visual identity guidelines, communication principles, digital presence standards, content strategy, photography requirements, and long-term positioning objectives for Carlos David Duarte.

The purpose is to establish a consistent professional identity across LinkedIn, GitHub, davdav.tech, email communications, conference participation, publications, and future architecture leadership opportunities.
