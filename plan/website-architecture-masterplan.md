# Executive Summary

The purpose of **davdav.tech** is not to function merely as a portfolio website.

The objective is to create a professional digital ecosystem positioning Carlos David Duarte as:

- Senior Software Engineer
- Technical Lead
- Application Owner
- Cloud Professional
- Solution Architect (Career Trajectory)
- Technology Thought Leader

The platform should become the digital headquarters of Carlos's professional identity and evolve over multiple years.
