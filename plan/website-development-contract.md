# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business objectives, technical requirements, design principles, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and governance rules for the development of the professional website and digital platform of Carlos David Duarte.

The platform will serve as a long-term digital presence supporting professional branding, technical content publication, networking, recruiting visibility, and thought leadership.

---

# 2. Project Vision

Create a modern, scalable, high-performance professional platform that positions Carlos David Duarte as:

- Senior Software Engineer
- Technical Lead
- Application Owner
- Cloud Professional
- Emerging Solution Architect

The website must complement LinkedIn while providing greater control over branding, content, and professional communication.
